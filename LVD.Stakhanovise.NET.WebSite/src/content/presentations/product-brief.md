## Why this exists

Background jobs are the railways of modern services. Stakhanovise.NET provides a durable, observable queue and worker model for .NET teams that need predictable throughput without reinventing scheduling.

## Promise

- Reliable delivery across SQL Server or PostgreSQL backends.
- Simple worker APIs with strong defaults for retries and visibility timeouts.
- Observability baked in: metrics, logs, and health signals.

## Proof points

- Horizontal scale by pointing multiple worker pools at the same queue.
- Dead-letter handling and replay for poison messages.
- Tested under high contention with consistent throughput and fairness.
